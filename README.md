# 🏃 Run Forever

**Your fitness journey, simplified.**

Run Forever is a modern fitness tracking application designed to help you stay active, monitor your daily progress, and build healthier habits.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://run-forever.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Features

- 👟 **Step Tracking** – Monitor your daily steps and activity goals.
- 📍 **Distance Tracking** – Track distance covered while walking or running.
- 🔥 **Calorie Tracking** – Monitor estimated calories burned.
- ⏱️ **Run Timer** – Measure the duration of your workouts.
- 📊 **Activity History** – Review previous workouts.
- 📈 **Analytics Dashboard** – Explore weekly and monthly fitness trends.
- 🎯 **Goal Setting** – Set daily fitness targets and track progress.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** – Switch between light and dark themes.
- ✨ **Smooth Animations** – Enjoy fluid transitions and subtle interactions.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Experience a consistent interface across supported screens.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Run Forever follows a minimal, elegant, and user-first design philosophy inspired by Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.

- Clean layouts with generous whitespace.
- Soft gradients and subtle shadows.
- Rounded corners and modern typography.
- Intuitive navigation and clear visual hierarchy.
- Smooth transitions and micro-interactions.

---

## 📱 Screens

- **Home Dashboard** – Overview of steps, distance, calories, and goals.
- **Run Tracking** – Monitor workout duration and activity progress.
- **Activity History** – Review previous workouts.
- **Analytics & Insights** – Analyze fitness trends and performance.
- **Profile & Goals** – Manage personal preferences and fitness targets.
